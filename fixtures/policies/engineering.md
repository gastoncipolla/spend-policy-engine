# Harbor Lane Supply Co. — Engineering Expense Policy

Applies to every member of the Engineering team. All amounts are in US dollars (USD).
Harbor Lane Supply Co. is a fictional company used for demonstration only.

## 1. Meals

Meals are reimbursed up to USD 50.00 per expense.
Client meals require a reasonable business justification.

## 2. Travel

Ground transport (taxis, rideshare, trains) is limited to USD 100.00 per employee per week.
Ground transport is reimbursable Monday to Friday only; a weekend ride with a written justification goes to manager review.
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to USD 180.00 per night.
We prefer partner hotels whenever one is available.
Flights are reimbursed up to USD 700.00 per ticket.
Book economy class unless the flight is longer than 8 hours.

## 3. Software and training

Software subscriptions are limited to USD 300.00 per employee per calendar month.
Training courses and conferences are reimbursed up to USD 2,000.00 per expense.
Software or training expenses above USD 500.00 require a pre-approval ID from your manager.

## 4. Not reimbursable

Alcohol is not reimbursable.
Entertainment is not reimbursable for the Engineering team.
Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
