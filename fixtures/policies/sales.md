# Harbor Lane Supply Co. — Sales Expense Policy

Applies to every member of the Sales team. All amounts are in US dollars (USD).
Harbor Lane Supply Co. is a fictional company used for demonstration only.

## 1. Meals and entertainment

Meals are reimbursed up to USD 80.00 per expense.
Client entertainment is reimbursed up to USD 150.00 per expense.
Meals and entertainment are reimbursable Monday to Friday between 08:00 and 22:00.
Outside those days and hours, an expense with a written justification goes to manager review.
Client meals require a reasonable business justification.

## 2. Travel

Ground transport (taxis, rideshare, trains) is limited to USD 200.00 per employee per calendar month.
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to USD 250.00 per night.
We prefer partner hotels whenever one is available.
Flights are reimbursed up to USD 900.00 per ticket.
Book economy class unless the flight is longer than 8 hours.

## 3. Approvals

Any single expense above USD 80.00 requires a pre-approval ID from your manager.

## 4. Not reimbursable

Alcohol is not reimbursable.
Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
